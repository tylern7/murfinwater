About us

Water system experts for over six decades

Since 1959, Murfin Brothers Water Systems has helped bring pure water to residents and businesses throughout the Hamilton, Burlington and Oakville area. Our expertise has built a long-term reputation with homeowners and businesses across the region.

With fast project completion, friendly service and quality equipment, you can feel confident in our work. Whether you're replacing an old purification system or water softener, or planning a first-time installation, every job includes a warranty on both workmanship and product.

We carry equipment and supplies on hand, so you don't have to wait for parts to be shipped in.

FAQ

Common questions

What are the costs for my water solution?

There are a lot of variables that affect the cost of your water solution. That's why we offer a free in-home estimate and consultation, so we can accurately determine the best solution and all the costs involved.

Will you be able to fix my water problem?

We have many years of experience handling all kinds of water problems. Give us a call and we'll provide testimonials or referrals for the problems we've solved.

I have a water system, but I'm not sure how it works or if it's working. Can you help?

We're more than happy to help. We'll need to see your entire water system to explain how it works and assess whether it's doing its job.

Where is my well and where does my water go?

Our experience allows us to more accurately determine your well's location, and while we're on-site we can show you where your water is going.
